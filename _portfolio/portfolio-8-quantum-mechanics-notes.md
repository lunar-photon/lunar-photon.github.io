*Quantum Mechanics for People like me* is a comprehensive, mathematically rigorous treatise on quantum mechanics constructed with zero algebraic compression. Every calculation, commutator, spectral theorem, and operator identity is written out in complete detail, developed to build mathematical and physical clarity from first principles without skipping steps.

Topics covered include:
- **Mathematical Foundations:** Linear algebra, Hilbert and Rigged Hilbert spaces, Schwarz inequality converting normed spaces to metric spaces, the Gelfand–Maurin nuclear spectral theorem, Dirac bra-ket calculus, and the statistical justification of expectation values.
- **Quantum Dynamics and Pictures:** Schr\"odinger, Heisenberg, and Interaction pictures, Ehrenfest's theorem, the classical-quantum correspondence, and why $\hat{H} = \hat{T} + \hat{V}$ does not imply $E = T + V$.
- **Exact Solvable Systems:** Harmonic oscillator operator dualities ($a^\dagger a$ vs. $a a^\dagger$), coherent and squeezed states, delta-function potentials, finite wells, and wavepacket dispersion.
- **Angular Momentum and Symmetries:** Lie algebra $\mathfrak{su}(2)$, representation theory, Pauli matrices, spin addition, Clebsch–Gordan coefficients, and sequential Stern--Gerlach filters as quantum Markov processes.
- **Central Potentials and Hydrogen:** Runge--Lenz vector, dynamical $SO(4)$ symmetry, Coulomb wavefunctions in spherical and parabolic coordinates, and momentum-space wavefunctions.
- **Relativistic Quantum Mechanics and Identical Particles:** The Dirac equation, gamma-matrix algebra, Gordon decomposition, negative-energy states, Klein paradox, permutation operator $\hat{P}_{12}$, simultaneous diagonalization with $\hat{H}$ giving $\lambda = \pm 1$, bosons, fermions, and the Pauli exclusion principle.
- **Perturbation Theory and Atomic Hierarchy:** Non-degenerate and degenerate perturbation theory, relativistic mass corrections, spin-orbit ($LS$) coupling derived from the electron's rest frame ($B_{\text{int}}$), anomalous Zeeman effect and Land\'e $g$-factor, Paschen--Back effect, hyperfine structure and the 21 cm hydrogen line, Stark effect, and the master hierarchy of atomic interactions.
- **Time-Dependent Perturbation and Open Systems:** Dyson series, Fermi's Golden Rule, the classical Lorentz oscillator model of radiation reaction, spontaneous emission, the Weisskopf--Wigner Hamiltonian derivation of decaying states with complex energy, non-unitary time evolution under classical radiation, and line broadening from environmental perturbations via the energy--time uncertainty principle.
- **WKB Approximation and Scattering Theory:** Connection formulas, Gamow's theory of alpha decay and Geiger--Nuttall law, Lippmann--Schwinger equation, Born approximation, partial wave analysis, phase shifts, Breit--Wigner resonances, and the optical theorem.
