Detailed personal notes on conformal field theory, developed alongside coursework and research. The notes focus on reconstructing derivations from standard references in a more explicit and self-contained way, particularly in areas where intermediate steps are often omitted in textbooks — with the aim of building clarity in the structure of the theory through explicit calculation.

Based primarily on "The Yellow Book" (Di Francesco, Mathieu & Sénéchal, *Conformal Field Theory*).
