Self-contained notes for readers who know linear algebra and undergraduate quantum mechanics. They explain why the tensor-product picture of a subsystem breaks down for quantum fields, many-body systems and gravity, and develop its replacement from the beginning: von Neumann algebras, the GNS construction, the type I/II/III classification, Tomita–Takesaki modular theory, and the crossed product. The second half applies these tools to AdS/CFT, subregion–subalgebra duality, entanglement islands, and simple models of quantum gravity.

Every new construction is checked against a calculation from ordinary quantum mechanics, and every step of every calculation is numbered and explained. The logical development follows H. Liu, *Lectures on entanglement, von Neumann algebras, and emergence of spacetime* (arXiv:2510.07017).
