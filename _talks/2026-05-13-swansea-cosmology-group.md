Invited Zoom talk given to the Swansea Cosmology Group, presenting work on evolving cosmological correlators from the end of inflation to the CMB sky through reheating, and the resulting imprints of early-universe particle production on cosmological observables. Based on arXiv:2603.24635 (with Debaprasad Maity).
