This paper generalizes the brick wall method—originally developed for spherically symmetric black holes—to stationary axisymmetric spacetimes. The method provides a systematic way to compute black hole entropy by regulating quantum field modes near the horizon, extending the thermodynamic description to rotating black hole geometries.
