A reading path for students working toward quantum field theory in curved spacetime, holography, and early-universe cosmology. Roughly ordered from foundational to advanced within each area; not every book needs to be read cover to cover, but working through derivations by hand rather than skimming is what actually builds the intuition. For worked-through derivations and notes on many of these topics, see my [Notes]({{ base_path }}/portfolio/).

## Quantum Mechanics & Quantum Field Theory

* *Quantum Mechanics: Fundamentals* — Gottfried, Yan
* *Advanced Quantum Mechanics* — Sakurai
* *Quantum Theory of Fields, Vol. I–III* — Weinberg
* *QFT and the Standard Model* — Schwartz
* *Finite Temperature Field Theory* — Ashok Das
* *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information* — Nielsen, Chuang

## Classical Mechanics & Mathematical Methods

* *Classical Mechanics* — Goldstein, Poole, Safko
* *Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos* — Strogatz
* *Mathematical Methods for Physicists* — Arfken, Weber, Harris
* *Lie Algebras in Particle Physics* — Georgi
* *Lie Groups, Physics, and Geometry* — Gilmore
* *Topology* — Munkres
* *Differential Topology and Quantum Field Theory* — Nash

## Gauge Theory & Particle Physics

* *Introduction to Elementary Particles* — Griffiths
* *Gauge Field Theory* — Bailin, Love
* *Quantization of Gauge Systems* — Henneaux, Teitelboim
* *Supersymmetry and Supergravity* — Buchbinder, Kuzenko
* *Physics of Neutrino Interactions* — Athar, Singh

## Electrodynamics & Statistical Mechanics

* *Introduction to Electrodynamics* — Griffiths
* *Classical Electrodynamics* — Greiner
* *Statistical Mechanics* — Pathria, Beale
* *Fluid Mechanics* — Lifshitz, Pitaevskii

## General Relativity, Cosmology & Black Holes

* *Spacetime and Geometry* — Carroll
* *Gravitation and Cosmology* — Weinberg
* *Gravitation* — Misner, Thorne, Wheeler
* *Primordial Cosmology* — Peter, Uzan
* *The Mathematical Theory of Black Holes* — Chandrasekhar
* *Covariant Quantum Gravity* — Percacci

## String Theory & Conformal Field Theory

* *Conformal Field Theory* — Di Francesco, Mathieu, Sénéchal
* *String Theory, Vol. I–II* — Polchinski

## Notices

**Watch out!** These are the texts I actually used to build the background for quantum field theory in curved spacetime and inflationary cosmology — they are dense, and skipping the exercises defeats the purpose.
