    <h1>{{ site.author.name }}</h1>
    <p class="lead">I study questions at the intersection of cosmology, quantum field theory, and gravity.</p>
    <p>My research interests include the cosmic microwave background (CMB), early universe cosmology, field theory, and black holes. I am particularly interested in the quantum mechanical frameworks used to describe the early universe, and in how its physics can leave observable signatures in the CMB.</p>
    <p>My work has explored cosmological correlation functions, quantum fields in curved spacetime, conformal symmetry, and black hole thermodynamics. I completed an MSc in Physics at IIT Guwahati.</p>
